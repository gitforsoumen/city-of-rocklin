Robin TrowerQuarry Park Amphitheater

Robin Trower brings legendary blues-rock to Quarry Park – Sept 25
Guitar legend Robin Trower is lighting up the Quarry Park Amphitheatre stage on Thursday, September 25, for a night of soulful, electrifying blues-rock. With a career spanning over half a century—starting with Procol Harum and soaring through iconic solo releases like Bridge of Sighs—Trower remains a powerhouse of guitar artistry.

Expect to hear his signature tunes—like the thrilling “Day of the Eagle,” the classic “Too Rolling Stoned,” and the haunting “Bridge of Sighs”—all brought to life with raw passion and masterful tone. Plus, fans will get a taste of his newest studio work, Come and Find Me, featuring the recently released single “I Would Lose My Mind,” out now.

This intimate rock-and-roll evening under the stars is a rare opportunity to experience a true guitar icon up close. Tickets are available now—claim yours and prepare for a night of unforgettable riffs and atmosphere at Quarry Park.

Get access to presale tickets on Wednesday June 11 at 10am by using the code ROCKLIN.
Main sale tickets go on sale Friday, June 13th at 10am!

  • Quarry Park Amphitheater, 4000 Rocklin Road
  • Thursday, Sept. 25, 2025
  • Doors open at 6 PM, show starts at 7 PM
